# Exact metadata for references actually used

The manuscript depends on exactly two external mathematical results.

1. Henri Darmon and Loïc Merel, “Winding quotients and some variants of
   Fermat's Last Theorem,” *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik*
   **490** (1997), 81–100. DOI:
   <https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1997.490.81>. The used statement is part 1 of
   the Main Theorem: for exponent `n>=3`, `x^n+y^n=2z^n` has no nontrivial
   primitive integer solution. With `n=3`, this excludes a nonconstant
   three-term rational AP of cubes after the common-cube denominator clearing
   stated in the manuscript.

2. Lajos Hajdu and Szabolcs Tengely, “Powers in arithmetic progressions,”
   *The Ramanujan Journal* **55** (2021), 965–986. DOI:
   <https://doi.org/10.1007/s11139-020-00331-5>. The used result is Theorem 2
   specialized to `N=5`, which gives `P_5(3)=3` for ordinary cubes. The
   publisher records publication on 30 January 2021 and issue date August 2021.

No theorem of Helfgott, Venkatesh, or another author is used in the proof, so
such references are not included merely as background decoration. Related-work
search hits are recorded separately in `prior_art_search.md`.
