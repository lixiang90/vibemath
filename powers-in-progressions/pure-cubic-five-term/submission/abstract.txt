For a nonconstant rational five-term arithmetic progression, we ask how many
nonzero terms can, after multiplication by one common rational scalar, be cubes
in a single nontrivial pure cubic field. We prove that the exact maximum is
four. The proof determines the rational Kummer kernel, obtains exactly 25
five-position color orbits, and reduces 15 residual orbits to 60 explicit
systems of diagonal cubic equations. Exhaustive finite-field enumeration gives
a good-prime obstruction for every system. The progression (-3,-1,1,3,5)
supplies the lower bound over Q(cuberoot(3)). Six distinct four-hit
color/position models additionally have positive-rank genus-one constructions
and hence infinite families. A seventh model, ((0,1,2,4),0102), has the
explicit nondegenerate progression (64,36,8,-20,-48) over Q(cuberoot(6));
its natural elliptic quotient has a certified non-torsion point. No infinite
family on this genus-four source is claimed. Thus seven of the initial 31
four-hit models are closed for existence and 24 remain open. The 25 Round-09
reuse clusters are certified only under explicit coordinate permutations, not
under arbitrary Q-isomorphisms.
