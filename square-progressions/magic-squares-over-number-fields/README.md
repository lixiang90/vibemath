# Magic squares over number fields

This is an active long-term research record concerning three-by-three magic
squares whose entries are squares in low-degree algebraic number fields, with
special attention to quadratic and cubic fields and to elliptic-curve
reformulations.

The files contain verified algebraic identities, searches, examples, and
literature notes.  They do not prove existence of a nondegenerate square of
squares over a quadratic or cubic field unless a statement is explicitly
proved in the corresponding note and test.  Search exhaustion outside a stated
box is never implied.
