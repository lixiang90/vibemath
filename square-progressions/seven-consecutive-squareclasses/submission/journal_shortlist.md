# Journal shortlist and policy gate

Policy and scope review date: **2026-09-03**.  This is a scope assessment, not
a prediction of acceptance.  Every venue's instructions, templates,
AI-disclosure rules and data policy must be rechecked on the day of submission.

## Excluded venue: INTEGERS

Official policy: [INTEGERS, Instructions for Submitting
Articles](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/submit.html).

INTEGERS is **not an eligible venue for this project**.  Its official
instructions, checked on 2026-09-03, say that the journal will not consider an
article that uses artificial intelligence to produce mathematics, computer
code, bibliographic information, or other content (apart from basic
grammar/spelling assistance).  This project materially used AI assistance in
several of those categories.  Disclosure cannot cure a direct eligibility
conflict, so the manuscript must not be submitted to INTEGERS.

## First choice: Research in Number Theory

Official journal page: [Research in Number
Theory](https://link.springer.com/journal/40993).

The official scope covers number theory and arithmetic geometry.  The paper's
finite squareclass classification, explicit genus-one quartics, integral-point
proofs and reproducible descent-style reductions fit that scope.  A submission
should explain why reducing the canonical universe to 7 necessary patterns is
a standalone advance even though the paper does not decide `R_2(7)`.

## Backup 1: Journal of Number Theory

Official aims and scope: [Journal of Number Theory — Aims and
Scope](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-number-theory/about/aims-and-scope).

This is a broader number-theory venue.  The Diophantine and arithmetic-
geometric content is relevant, but the authors should reassess whether the
paper's present length and depth meet the journal's current editorial standard.
The current AI, data, source-file and cover-letter policies must be checked on
the official site immediately before submission.

## Backup 2, conditional: Electronic Journal of Combinatorics

Official site: [The Electronic Journal of
Combinatorics](https://www.combinatorics.org/).

This venue is appropriate only if the submitted version foregrounds the exact
finite orbit/classification theorem and its discrete-mathematical significance;
the arithmetic geometry should remain essential rather than decorative.  Its
current author and AI policies must be checked before choosing it.
