# Round 11: exact isogeny Selmer groups and a rank upper bound

Date: 2026-09-04.

## Theorem closed in this round

For

    E : y^2=x^3-591895071*x^2+58536289153843200*x,
    E': y^2=x^3+1183790142*x^2+116194618458722241*x,

with the standard degree-two isogeny `phi:E->E'` having kernel generated by
`(0,0)` and dual isogeny `hat(phi):E'->E`, the exact isogeny Selmer groups
are

    Sel^(hat(phi))(E'/Q) = {1,3,5,7,15,21,35,105} = <3,5,7>,
    Sel^phi(E/Q) = {1,4230241,339106321,1434501462453361}
                 = <4230241,339106321>.

Their dimensions over `F_2` are `3` and `2`. Consequently
`rank E(Q) <= 3`. This is an upper bound, not a rank computation.

## Why the local lists are exact Selmer groups

For `E_(a,b):y^2=x^3+a*x^2+b*x`, the Kummer class represented by `d` has
covering

    N^2=d*U^4+a*U^2*V^2+(b/d)*V^4.

The support lemma shows that a squarefree representative is supported only on
`-1` and the primes dividing `b`. The two sides therefore have 32 signed
classes each. The complete place set is
`S={infinity,2,3,5,7,59,71699,339106321}`.

Outside `S`, the binary-quartic discriminant is a unit; the smooth genus-one
reduction has an `F_p`-point by Hasse and it lifts by Hensel. At the places
in `S`, the Round-09 and Round-10 uniform local theorems exclude every class
outside the two displayed sets. For every surviving class, the existing
512-cell certificate supplies a positive local witness at every finite bad
place; Round 11 re-evaluates all 84 such square tests and checks all twelve
real rows. Thus no unchecked prime or bounded negative search is hidden in
the conclusion.

## Rank calculation and convention check

The symbolic audit verifies the standard maps, their composition with the
dual, and the covering-to-Kummer map. With the conventions above,

    E'(Q)/phi E(Q)       -> Sel^phi(E/Q),
    E(Q)/hat(phi) E'(Q) -> Sel^(hat(phi))(E'/Q)

are injections. Factoring multiplication by two through the two isogenies
gives

    2^rank = |E'(Q)/phi E(Q)| * |E(Q)/hat(phi) E'(Q)|
             / (|E(Q)[phi]| * |E'(Q)[hat(phi)]|).

The two kernel orders are both two, while the Selmer-size product is
`8*4=32`. Therefore `2^rank<=32/4=8`, proving `rank<=3`.

## Boundary

compute the full 2-Selmer group or a Cassels--Tate pairing, or decide whether
Campbell's ninth point exists. It does not claim an independent second-CAS
