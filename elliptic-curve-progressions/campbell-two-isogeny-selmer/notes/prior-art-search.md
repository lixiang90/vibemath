## Source and neighbouring literature

The exact source of the family is Garikai Campbell,
[*A Note on Arithmetic Progressions on Elliptic Curves*](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL6/Campbell/campbell4.html),
Journal of Integer Sequences 6 (2003), Article 03.1.3. Corollary 2.4 and
Theorem 2.5 give the parameter change, the four coefficients of `g_m`, the
curve `D`, and the eight x-coordinates `0,...,7`. Campbell does not state the
specific ninth-value quartic `H`, its Jacobian's two isogeny Selmer groups, or
the present 512-cell local classification.

Nearby work includes Bremner's eight-term construction, Garcia-Selfa--Tornero
on simultaneous progressions, and Fisher's binary-quartic descent and pairing
formulae. These are method/context sources, not evidence that the numerical
finite theorem has or has not appeared elsewhere.
