# Journal shortlist and policy gate

Policy check date: 2026-09-03. This is a scope assessment, not an acceptance
prediction. Recheck the journal-specific rules on the day of submission.

## First choice: Research in Number Theory

The two-isogeny descent, binary quartic, exact local classification, and
explicit arithmetic geometry are directly within scope. Springer Nature's
current [AI guidance for researchers](https://group.springernature.com/gp/group/ai/ai-guidance-for-our-researchers-and-communities)
permits AI-assisted work subject to human accountability and transparent
declaration when AI helped generate text, analysis, or content. Before
submission, the human authors must verify the journal's current article-level
instructions, approve the exact disclosure, and explain why the finite
one-curve theorem is a useful standalone calculation.

## Backup: Journal of Number Theory

This Elsevier journal is a natural arithmetic-geometry venue. Elsevier's
current [generative-AI policy for journal authors](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals)
allows responsible AI support with human oversight and requires a declaration
of tool name, purpose, scope, and author review. The authors must confirm that
the journal's current Guide for Authors adds no stricter rule and must place
research-process use in the Methods/reproducibility discussion as required.

## Explicitly excluded under the present workflow

**INTEGERS is not an eligible target.** Its current author instructions say
that papers using AI to generate mathematics, code, bibliographic information,
or other content will not be considered. That conflicts with the factual
inventory in `ai_disclosure.md`; the conflict must not be hidden by changing
the disclosure. See the [INTEGERS submission instructions](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/submit.html).

**Journal of Integer Sequences is also excluded for the present manuscript.**
Its current instructions prohibit LLM-written manuscript prose and require
explicit in-paper credit for LLM-suggested proof ideas; its sequence-focused
scope is additionally weak for this draft. See the
[JIS submission instructions](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/).
